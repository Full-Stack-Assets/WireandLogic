Trump Media launched the Truth API in early August, offering subscribers a low‑latency feed of posts from its Truth Social platform. More than ten high‑frequency trading firms have signed up, paying between $60,000 and $100,000 per month for the service. The offering targets market‑moving statements, especially those from former president Donald Trump, whose posts can sway stock prices. The deal arrives as Trump Media reports a $238 million loss for the April‑June quarter and seeks new revenue streams. Regulators and ethicists are watching closely because the service monetizes political communication.

<Callout type="takeaway">Fast access to Trump’s posts is becoming a paid commodity, giving traders a potential edge while raising ethical concerns.</Callout>

## What happened
The Truth API went live at the beginning of August, promising “first sight” of posts from influential Truth Social accounts. Interim CEO Kevin McGurn told investors that early customers—mostly high‑frequency trading firms—are being charged between $60,000 and $100,000 a month for the feed.

Trump Media expects the service to become a “meaningful” and “durable” revenue stream, complementing its advertising and digital‑asset businesses. The company also mentioned exploring sales to technology firms, news organisations, and betting markets, signaling a broader monetisation push amid a $238 million quarterly loss.

## Why it matters
Providing a paid, low‑latency channel for political statements blurs the line between public communication and proprietary market data, potentially giving paying firms an informational advantage. The model raises legal questions about whether profiting from a sitting president’s posts constitutes unfair market practice. It also highlights Trump Media’s reliance on non‑core revenue as its crypto ventures falter, making the Truth API a litmus test for the company’s long‑term viability.

## How to think about it
If you are a trader, evaluate whether the marginal edge from a few seconds of earlier information outweighs the subscription cost and potential compliance risk. For product teams, consider building similar low‑latency feeds for other high‑impact sources, but embed robust governance to avoid conflicts of interest. Investors should watch how the service scales and whether regulators intervene, as those outcomes will affect Trump Media’s broader financial outlook.

<Callout type="warning">Do not rely on the Truth API as a shortcut to insider information; ensure all trading activity complies with securities laws and your firm’s compliance policies.</Callout>
