OpenRouter announced today that the GPT-5.6 Sol model’s API pricing has been reduced by half. The change drops the per‑token cost from its previous level to a new, 50 % lower rate. The adjustment applies to all existing and new OpenRouter accounts starting immediately. For developers building on the latest GPT model, the lower price could reshape budgeting decisions.

## What happened
On August 17, 2026, OpenRouter posted an update on its pricing page indicating that the GPT-5.6 Sol endpoint will now cost 50 % less than before.

The reduction applies uniformly across the tiered usage plan, and the model’s performance benchmarks remain unchanged, preserving its speed and token limits.
